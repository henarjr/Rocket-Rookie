# Rocket Rookie 🚀

**Rocket Rookie** is a 2D space survival game built with Unity. Take control of a little rocket, navigate through dangerous space, and dodge incoming obstacles for as long as you can.

Space is full of surprises, and one collision is all it takes to end your run. How long can you survive? 🌌

## 🎮 Gameplay

Guide your rocket through space, avoid obstacles, and survive as long as possible to beat your high score.

## 🕹️ How to Play

- **Hold Left Mouse Button** — Move your rocket.
- **Move your mouse** — Guide the rocket's direction.
- **Avoid obstacles** — Don't let your rocket crash.
- **Survive longer** — Keep going to increase your score!

## ✨ Features

- 2D arcade-style space survival gameplay.
- Mouse-controlled rocket movement.
- Obstacles to dodge while navigating through space.
- Score tracking based on survival.
- High-score system.
